# DALC Experiences System

## Purpose

The experiences module handles activities and leisure experiences across Dubai.

These are not mobility services.

Examples

yacht charter  
jet ski  
desert safari  
skydiving  
dining experiences  
nightlife  
helicopter tours

## Folder Structure

src/components/experiences  
src/pages/experiences  
src/data/experiences

## Categories

Water Activities  
Desert Activities  
City Tours  
Dining Experiences  
Nightlife  
Air Activities

## Core Goal

Allow users to discover and request experiences in Dubai.

Requests are handled by DALC concierge system.
