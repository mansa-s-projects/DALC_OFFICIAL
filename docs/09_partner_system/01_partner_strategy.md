# Partner Strategy

## Partner Thesis

DALC should build a partner network that increases execution capacity without giving away the customer relationship.

Partners are not the product. Partners are fulfillment infrastructure.

## Which Partner Types Matter Most First

| Partner type | Why they matter early |
| --- | --- |
| Business setup and legal operators | Core to founder and relocation monetization |
| Relocation support partners | Core to the launch wedge |
| Premium transport providers | Strong repeat usage and reliable revenue |
| Stays and hospitality partners | High ticket value and attachment revenue |
| Experience and nightlife operators | Useful for lifestyle monetization after trust exists |
| Schooling consultants | Important in family relocation flows |
| Agencies and intermediaries | Strong referral leverage once operations stabilize |

## Why Each Partner Joins

Partners join DALC when DALC offers one of four things:

1. qualified premium demand
2. better conversion than generic inquiry sources
3. repeat high-value customers
4. reduced sales friction through structured demand

## How Each Partner Gets Value

| Partner type | DALC value to partner |
| --- | --- |
| Business setup firms | Higher-intent founder demand and packaged workflows |
| Relocation partners | Better-qualified cases with clearer user context |
| Transport providers | Premium demand with lower acquisition effort |
| Stays providers | Curated higher-value bookings and extensions |
| Experience operators | Better conversion on premium, coordinated plans |
| Agencies | Reliable local execution partner |

## Economics DALC Should Offer

DALC should use the simplest viable model per category.

| Model | Best use case |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | Standard bookable or attributable categories |
| Referral fee | Partner-led delivery where DALC originates demand |
| Markup | DALC-packaged premium execution |
| White-label fee | Agencies or B2B partners needing DALC as execution infrastructure |

DALC should avoid bespoke partner economics at launch unless the volume is meaningful.

## How DALC Avoids Being Bypassed

Bypass risk is one of the biggest structural threats.

DALC should reduce bypass through:

1. controlled request and quote flow
2. centralized payment capture where possible
3. clear attribution and partner agreements
4. user relationship continuity inside DALC, not just on partner channels
5. better post-sale coordination than the partner can offer alone

If DALC only forwards contact details, it will lose the customer relationship quickly.

## Quality Control

Every partner should be graded on:

1. response speed
2. fulfillment reliability
3. communication quality
4. pricing discipline
5. recovery behavior when things go wrong

Partners should not be treated equally. DALC should route more premium demand to the most reliable partners.

## SLA Expectations

| Partner type | Minimum expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Premium concierge or urgent suppliers | Fast response and strong same-day coordination |
| Business and relocation partners | Reliable status updates and predictable document handling |
| Hospitality and booking partners | Availability clarity and fast confirmation |

DALC should enforce SLAs internally even before legal paperwork becomes sophisticated.

## Reporting And Attribution

DALC should track for every partner:

1. requests assigned
2. quotes sent
3. bookings closed
4. revenue generated
5. failure rate
6. average response time
7. repeat user retention from partner-linked flows

## Retention Mechanisms

Partner retention should come from:

1. consistent quality demand
2. clear economics
3. predictable communication
4. transparent performance expectations
5. evidence that DALC is building a high-value customer base, not random traffic

## Launch Partner Priority List

1. business setup and legal operators
2. relocation support partners
3. premium transport providers
4. hospitality and stays partners
5. experience and nightlife partners
6. schooling consultants
7. agencies and intermediaries

## Outbound Partner Pitch

DALC should say:

**We are building the execution layer for high-intent Dubai demand. We are not sending random leads. We are qualifying user intent, structuring the request, and routing serious premium demand to partners who can actually deliver. If you want better customers instead of more noise, that is where DALC creates value.**
